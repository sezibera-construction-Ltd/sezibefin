How we hold quality

Our quality management system follows six basic principles, applied on every project regardless of size.

01  Established processes

Provide for long-term quality control through processes that are already proven, not invented per job.

02  Quality culture

Nurture and guide our quality culture through proven policies.

03  Control the standard

Successfully identify and control the quality standard the project is held to.

04  Plan for quality

Identify up front the measures by which the required quality will be achieved.

05  Ensure quality

Encourage all parties on the project to work together to deliver it.

06  Continuous improvement

Provide a process of continuous improvement, correcting any potential defect.
